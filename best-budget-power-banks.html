How much power bank capacity do you need?

5,000mAh: Light and occasional use

A compact power bank can be convenient for emergencies and short outings. It has less stored energy than a 10,000mAh or 20,000mAh model. Compare size, weight and output before choosing one.

10,000mAh: Everyday carrying

This capacity can offer a useful balance between portability and stored energy for many phone users. It is worth considering if you carry a charger in your pocket or small bag.

20,000mAh: Longer days and travel

A 20,000mAh power bank provides more advertised capacity and can be useful for long workdays or charging multiple devices. It is generally larger and heavier than smaller alternatives.

Remember that advertised mAh is not the exact amount delivered to your phone. Voltage conversion, power losses, battery condition and usage affect real-world charging results.

What does charging wattage mean?

Wattage indicates the rate at which a power bank can supply power under supported conditions. A higher advertised number does not automatically mean faster charging for every phone or laptop.

Before buying, check:

  • The maximum output of the specific port you will use.
  • Whether your device supports USB-C Power Delivery, PPS or another required charging protocol.
  • Whether the correct cable is included or needs to be purchased separately.
  • How much power remains available when charging more than one device.

USB-C or USB-A: Which should you choose?

USB-C is common on newer phones, tablets and laptops and can support higher charging power when both devices and the cable are compatible. USB-A remains useful for older cables and accessories.

Do not assume that every USB-C port supports the same speed. Read the port specifications and confirm whether each port supports charging the power bank, charging other devices, or both.

  • Paying for high wattage that your devices cannot use.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 20,000mAh power bank worth buying?

It can be useful for long days away from a wall outlet, travel and charging multiple devices. If you mainly need occasional top-ups, a smaller model may be lighter and more convenient.

How many times can a 20,000mAh power bank charge a phone?

The number varies with your phone's battery, power conversion losses, the power bank's usable energy and how the phone is used while charging. Do not treat the advertised capacity as an exact count of phone charges.

Does a higher wattage power bank charge every phone faster?

No. The phone must support the charging protocol and power level. Charging speed can also vary with temperature, battery level, cable and whether other devices are connected.

Can I take a power bank on an aeroplane?

Power banks are subject to airline and aviation safety requirements. Check the latest rules with your airline before departure, including capacity limits and where the battery may be carried.

Should I buy a 10,000mAh or 20,000mAh power bank?

Consider 10,000mAh if portability matters most. Consider 20,000mAh if you need more stored energy for longer days or multiple devices. Check the actual weight and dimensions before ordering.
