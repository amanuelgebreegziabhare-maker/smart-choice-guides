How Much Power Bank Capacity Do You Need?

Capacity is usually advertised in milliamp-hours (mAh). A larger number generally indicates more stored charge, but it does not tell the whole story: voltage, conversion efficiency and your device's battery affect how much usable energy reaches the device.

5,000mAh

A small option for emergency top-ups and people who value a compact charger. Check the actual size, weight and output before choosing one.

10,000mAh

A practical middle ground for many phone users. It can provide more stored energy than a compact model without the size of some higher-capacity options.

20,000mAh

Worth considering for travel, long workdays or charging more than one device. These models are generally larger and heavier, so check dimensions and weight.

Why Charging Speed Matters

Capacity tells you how much energy is stored; output wattage helps indicate how quickly a compatible device may charge. Check both the maximum output and supported charging standards.

A higher-wattage power bank does not automatically charge every device faster. The phone, tablet or other device must support the relevant charging protocol and power level. USB-C Power Delivery can be useful for compatible devices.

USB-C vs USB-A Power Banks

USB-C is widely used on modern phones, tablets and accessories, and can support higher charging power when the power bank and device are compatible. USB-A can still be useful for older cables and accessories.

Before buying, check whether the USB-C port supports both input and output, how much output power it provides, and whether the package includes a cable. A port's shape alone does not guarantee a particular charging speed.

Choosing a Power Bank for Travel

For travel, balance capacity with weight and convenience. Consider these points before packing a power bank:

  • Capacity and the battery rating shown on the product
  • Weight and physical dimensions
  • USB-C input/output and supported charging speed
  • Number and type of ports
  • Airline rules and destination requirements

Check the latest rules with your airline before departure. Requirements can vary, and power banks are commonly subject to restrictions on where they may be carried. For more ideas, read our Best Travel Tech Gadgets guide.

What to Check Before Buying

  • Capacity: Choose enough for your routine without carrying unnecessary weight.
  • Charging output: Match supported wattage and charging standards to your devices.
  • Ports: Check the number of ports and whether each supports input, output or both.
  • Size and weight: Look at the actual dimensions, not just the capacity printed on the box.
  • Build quality and support: Review the manufacturer's specifications, warranty and customer support options.
  • Price: Compare current prices and features rather than choosing by brand name alone.

Common Power Bank Buying Mistakes

  • Choosing the largest capacity automatically: More capacity can mean more weight and bulk.
  • Ignoring charging compatibility: Confirm that your device supports the power bank's charging standards.
  • Assuming every USB-C port is equally fast: Check the rated input and output specifications.
  • Buying on price alone: Consider product details, warranty and seller reliability as well.
  • Forgetting portability: A charger that is inconvenient to carry may not suit everyday use.

Budget vs More Expensive Power Banks

A budget power bank may be sufficient for occasional phone charging. Paying more can make sense when you need faster USB-C Power Delivery, greater capacity, additional ports or better product support.

Compare the actual specifications, warranty and current price rather than choosing by brand or capacity alone. The best value is the model that meets your needs without making you pay for features you will not use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 20,000mAh power bank worth it?

It can be useful for travel, long workdays and charging multiple compatible devices. It is generally larger and heavier than lower-capacity models, so consider how you will carry it.

How many times can a power bank charge a phone?

The result depends on the power bank's usable energy, your phone's battery, conversion losses and how you use the phone while charging. The advertised mAh figure is not the exact amount delivered to your phone.

Is USB-C better for a power bank?

USB-C can be convenient for modern devices and can support faster charging when the power bank and connected device use compatible standards.

Should I buy a 10,000mAh or 20,000mAh power bank?

Consider 10,000mAh if portability is a priority. Consider 20,000mAh if you need more stored energy for travel or longer periods away from an outlet.

Can I travel with a power bank?

Power banks are subject to airline and aviation safety rules. Check the latest requirements with your airline before travelling, including restrictions on capacity and where the power bank may be carried.
