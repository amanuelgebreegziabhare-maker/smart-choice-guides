Best AI Tools for Creating YouTube Shorts

A practical guide to using AI for ideas, scripts, voiceovers, video editing, captions, thumbnails and YouTube Shorts production.

YouTube Shorts can be one of the easiest ways for a new creator to reach an audience. The challenge is producing good content consistently without spending hours every day writing scripts, recording audio, editing videos and creating thumbnails.

AI tools can help speed up many parts of the process. They can generate ideas, help create scripts, clean up writing, produce voiceovers, edit videos, generate captions and assist with visual content.

However, you do not need dozens of AI subscriptions. A simple workflow using a few reliable tools can be enough to produce consistent Shorts.

In this guide, we look at practical AI tools that can help with different stages of YouTube Shorts production.

1. ChatGPT — Ideas and Scripts

A general-purpose AI assistant can be useful at the beginning of the Shorts creation process.

What you can use it for

  • Generating video ideas
  • Creating hooks
  • Writing short scripts
  • Creating different versions of a script
  • Generating titles
  • Creating calls to action
  • Brainstorming content series

For example, instead of starting with a blank document, you can ask an AI assistant to generate 20 Shorts ideas around a specific topic and then select the strongest ideas for your channel.

Best for: Content ideas, hooks, scripts and brainstorming.

2. Canva — Thumbnails and Visual Content

Canva is useful for creators who need simple but professional-looking visual content.

What you can use it for

  • Shorts graphics
  • Thumbnail designs
  • Channel graphics
  • Text overlays
  • Social media posts
  • Simple video content

Its templates can reduce the amount of time required to create graphics from scratch.

Best for: Visual design and simple creator graphics.

3. CapCut — Short-Form Video Editing

CapCut is particularly useful for creators producing short-form videos for platforms such as YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

Useful features include

  • Video trimming
  • Automatic captions
  • Text overlays
  • Transitions
  • Effects
  • Music and sound editing
  • Short-form video templates

A major advantage of a short-form editor is that you can quickly turn raw clips into a vertical video designed for mobile viewers.

Best for: Editing and preparing short-form videos.

4. ElevenLabs — AI Voiceovers

AI voice-generation platforms can help creators produce narration without recording their own voice for every video.

Possible uses

  • Educational Shorts
  • Explainer videos
  • Storytelling content
  • Product explanations
  • Faceless video channels
  • Short educational content

Voice quality and available features vary between platforms and subscription plans, so creators should review the current terms and commercial-use conditions before publishing content commercially.

Best for: AI narration and voiceover production.

5. Descript — Editing by Text

Descript combines transcription with audio and video editing. Instead of working only with a traditional video timeline, creators can work with a transcript and edit spoken content more naturally.

What it can help with

  • Transcription
  • Video editing
  • Podcast clips
  • Removing unwanted sections
  • Creating clips from longer recordings

This can be especially useful when turning longer interviews, podcasts or presentations into multiple Shorts.

Best for: Creators repurposing longer videos into short clips.

6. InVideo — AI-Assisted Video Creation

AI-assisted video platforms can help transform an idea or written description into a starting point for a video.

Potential uses

  • Creating video drafts
  • Explainer videos
  • Marketing videos
  • Social media content
  • Story-based videos

These tools can be useful when you want to create a first draft quickly and then customize the result yourself.

Best for: Quickly creating video drafts from ideas or scripts.

7. Adobe Express — Quick Creator Content

Adobe Express provides tools for creating social media graphics, videos and other digital content.

Useful for

  • Social media graphics
  • Short videos
  • Text effects
  • Templates
  • Promotional content

It can be useful for creators who want a straightforward design and content-creation environment.

Best for: Social media content and simple visual production.

8. Grammarly — Improve Your Scripts

Even when AI generates your first draft, the final script should sound natural and clear.

Writing assistants can help identify grammar problems, improve clarity and make sentences easier to understand.

Useful for

  • Checking scripts
  • Improving clarity
  • Correcting grammar
  • Making text more concise
  • Improving calls to action

Best for: Editing and polishing written content.

If you want to compare more AI writing options, see our guide to the best AI writing tools for beginners.

9. Perplexity — Research and Fact Checking

Creators who make educational, technology, finance or informational Shorts need to pay particular attention to accuracy.

AI research tools can help you investigate a topic and identify sources that you can check before writing your final script.

AI-generated information should not automatically be treated as fact. For important claims, check the original source.

Best for: Research and finding information to investigate further.

10. Google Gemini — Ideas and Research Assistance

Google Gemini can also be useful for brainstorming, writing, summarizing information and developing content ideas.

Possible uses

  • Generating content ideas
  • Creating outlines
  • Brainstorming hooks
  • Summarizing information
  • Developing video concepts

Creators who already use Google's ecosystem may find it convenient as part of their content workflow.

Best for: General AI assistance and content brainstorming.

A Simple AI Workflow for Creating YouTube Shorts

You do not need to use every tool listed above. A simple workflow can be much more effective.

Step 1 — Find a Topic

Start with a topic that your target audience actually wants to watch.

Look at questions people ask, common problems, current trends and subjects related to your niche.

Step 2 — Create the Hook

The first few seconds of a Short are extremely important.

Your opening should immediately tell viewers why they should continue watching.

For example, you might start with a surprising fact, a problem, a question or a strong promise.

Step 3 — Write a Short Script

Use an AI assistant to create a concise script. Then edit it so that it sounds natural and matches your own style.

Step 4 — Create the Voiceover

You can record your own voice or use an AI voice platform where its terms allow your intended commercial use.

Step 5 — Edit the Video

Combine your voiceover, video clips, graphics, captions and music using a short-form video editor.

Step 6 — Add Captions

Many viewers watch short-form videos without sound, so clear captions can make your content easier to follow.

Step 7 — Publish and Analyze

After publishing, look at your analytics and identify which topics, hooks and formats perform best.

Use that information to improve your next videos instead of simply producing more videos without learning from the results.

A Beginner-Friendly AI Shorts Setup

If you are just starting, you do not need an expensive collection of subscriptions.

A simple setup could be:

  • ChatGPT or Gemini for ideas and scripts
  • Canva for graphics
  • CapCut for editing and captions
  • Your own voice or an appropriate AI voice tool for narration
  • YouTube Analytics to measure results

This setup keeps your production process relatively simple while giving you enough tools to create consistent content.

How Much Can AI Speed Up Shorts Production?

The amount of time saved depends on the type of content you produce.

A creator who normally spends several hours researching, writing and editing one video may be able to reduce some of that work by using AI-assisted tools.

However, faster production does not automatically mean better content.

The most successful workflow is usually a combination of AI assistance and human judgment.

AI can help you move faster, but you should still decide what is worth publishing.

Should You Use AI to Create Faceless YouTube Shorts?

Faceless Shorts can be produced without appearing on camera. This can make content creation more comfortable for people who do not want to record themselves.

Possible formats include:

  • Educational videos
  • Technology tips
  • AI tutorials
  • Product comparisons
  • Interesting facts
  • How-to videos
  • Screen-recording tutorials

The important factor is the value of the content, not simply whether the creator appears on camera.

Important: Do Not Let AI Make Your Content Generic

One of the biggest risks of AI-assisted content is producing videos that feel identical to thousands of other AI-generated videos.

Add your own perspective, examples, experiences, opinions and useful information.

Use AI as a production assistant rather than allowing it to completely determine your content.

Our Practical Recommendation

If you are a beginner, start with a small toolset rather than subscribing to everything at once.

A practical starting combination is:

  1. AI assistant for ideas and scripts
  2. Canva for simple graphics
  3. CapCut for editing and captions
  4. YouTube Analytics for performance analysis

Once you understand your workflow, add specialized tools only when they solve a specific problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI tool for YouTube Shorts?

There is no single best tool for every creator. A combination of an AI assistant, video editor and design tool can cover most beginner workflows.

Can AI create an entire YouTube Short?

Some AI platforms can automate large parts of the process, but reviewing and improving the final video is still important.

Can I make YouTube Shorts without showing my face?

Yes. Educational videos, tutorials, product explanations, screen recordings and narrated videos can all be created without appearing on camera.

Are AI-generated voices allowed on YouTube?

AI-generated voices can be used in appropriate content, but creators should follow YouTube's current policies and the terms of the voice provider they use.

Should I pay for AI tools when I am just starting?

Usually, start with free plans or trials where available. Upgrade when a paid feature provides enough value to justify the cost.

How many Shorts should I publish each month?

There is no universal number. Consistency and quality are generally more useful than publishing a large number of videos that provide little value.
